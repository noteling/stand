Finds a fact in your notes and, when the notes do not have it, beyond them. Call it whenever someone asks for a fact you do not already know (a number, a date, a name, a decision, a detail about a project, a meeting, a person or an order, how something in a system works) before you say you don't know. The answer usually arrives a few seconds later, and you then say it in your own words. If the reply is STILL LOOKING, the answer will come later.
