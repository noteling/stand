Call this every time you choose to say nothing: someone has finished speaking and it was not said to you, or you have nothing that needs saying, or a message from the stage manager needs no spoken reply. Calling it is how you stay silent. It replaces any acknowledgement: never say "okay", "noted", "nothing" or "silent" out loud. Never call it in answer to a question that was put to you: that gets a spoken answer, even if the answer is that you have nothing to add.
