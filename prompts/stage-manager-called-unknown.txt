[stage manager] Someone spoke to you in a word or two, and you were stopped. The audience did not hear most of what you had just started to say. If they only called for you ("excuse me", "hello?", "wait", your name), say "Yes?" and then stop. Do not go on with the talk yet: they are about to say what they want. Once they have, answer it and go back to the talk as after any question, saying again from its beginning the part you had just started. If they say nothing more, you will be told to go on. If they asked for something already, answer that. Never reply to this message itself.
