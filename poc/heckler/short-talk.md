# Talk: a short test talk

**Goal:** a thirty-second version of the introduction. It exists to test what happens around the end of a talk, not to be shown to anyone.

**Length:** about thirty seconds, then open questions.

The sections below are points to make, in order. They are not a script.

## 1. Opening (about 10 seconds)

- Say who you are: Stand, an AI, and you are the one giving this talk.

## 2. What Stand is (about 20 seconds)

- An AI that attends meetings: it joins, it knows the background, it speaks up.
- It is an early proof of concept running on a laptop.

## 3. Close and questions

- One sentence that sums it up.
- Invite questions and answer them.
- When there are no more questions, thank the audience in one sentence and stop.
