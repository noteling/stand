# How a question is answered

## While Stand is talking

1. Someone starts to speak. After about a second of their voice, Stand stops.
2. The page stays where it is: it is what the question is about.
3. Stand answers, and then goes on from the last words the audience heard.

## Looking it up

When the answer is not in the talk, Stand says "let me check" and asks its brain.

- From the notes, the answer comes in about a second.
- From the code, it takes three to seven seconds.
- When nothing has the answer, Stand says it does not know.

## Asking for a page

Anyone can ask to see a page again: "can you go back to the last page?"

1. Stand says a word, and asks for the page of that section.
2. The page goes up and is looked at.
3. Stand says it is up, and answers.

When the talk goes on, the page for its next section comes back by itself.
