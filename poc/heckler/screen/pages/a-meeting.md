# A meeting, from start to finish

What happens when Stand gives a talk in a call, one step after another. This page is long on purpose: it is here so that the page has to move down while Stand speaks.

## What happens, step by step

### Before the meeting

Stand's human writes two lines about the meeting: what to present, to whom, and for how long.

The brain then prepares the talk. It reads the documents and the code in the folder it was given and writes a plan of points, which takes a minute or two. The plan is a file, and its human can read it and change it before the meeting.

### Joining the call

Stand's human joins the call, as they always do, and starts Stand beside it.

Stand hears the room through the microphone and the other side of the call through the Mac's own sound. Its human shares the window with the pages. Stand does not join a call or share a window by itself yet.

### The talk, a section at a time

Stand gives the talk a section at a time. Before each section, the page that belongs to it is put up and looked at, and only then is Stand told to speak.

Inside a section the page moves down with the voice, so that what Stand is saying stays in view.

### When someone interrupts

Stand stops after about a second of someone else's voice. A cough or an "mm" is too short to stop it.

It answers the question, and the page stays where it is, because the page is what the question is about. Then Stand goes on from the last words the audience heard.

### A question it cannot answer

When the answer is not in the talk, Stand says "let me check" and asks its brain.

The brain looks in the notes first and then in the code. From the notes an answer comes in about a second, and from the code in three to seven seconds. When nothing has the answer, Stand says that it does not know.

### The end of the talk

Stand invites questions and answers them.

When there are no more questions it thanks the audience in one sentence and stops. Every session is kept on the Mac, with a recording, a transcript and what the meeting cost.
