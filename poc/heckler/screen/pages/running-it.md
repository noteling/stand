# Running Stand

## What you need

- A Mac with macOS 14.2 or later.
- A Gemini API key of your own, for the voice.
- Claude Code on that Mac, signed in, if Stand is to look things up.

## What it costs

| What | About |
|---|---|
| The talk in which Stand introduces itself, with questions | 20 cents |
| An hour of steady talk, by estimate | 5 to 10 dollars |

The cost goes with how often the voice takes a turn, not with the minutes.

## What leaves the Mac

- The meeting's sound goes to Google, under your key.
- What the brain reads goes to Anthropic, under the Claude Code login on that Mac.
- Nothing goes to the people who make Stand.
